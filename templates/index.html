How to export your chat from WhatsApp
iPhone (iOS)
  1. Open the chat or group in WhatsApp.
  2. Tap the contact/group name at the top.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Export Chat.
  4. Choose "Without Media".
  5. Save the .txt file to Files and upload it here.
Android
  1. Open the chat or group in WhatsApp.
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top-right.
  3. Select More → Export chat.
  4. Choose "Without media".
  5. Save or send the .txt file to upload here.
Tip: Larger group chats with multiple years of history yield the richest birthday directories!
